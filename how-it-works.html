The operating loop

One place to see what changed and what matters.

MerchantAlert is designed around a simple loop: reconcile the account, rank the issues, send the signal, keep the history.

01 · Reconcile

Make change visible.

A scheduled reconciliation looks for new disapprovals, limitations, and other issue state changes. Known issues do not keep interrupting the team unless their status changes.

Example signal

“3 new issues appeared since the last check. One affects products with 2,400 impressions in the recent window.”

Illustrative copy for the workflow, not a customer result.

02 · Prioritize

Start where the opportunity is.

Not every issue deserves the same urgency. The product uses available clicks and impressions context to make the queue more useful than a flat list.

A

Impact

Issues attached to meaningful product visibility rise to the top.

B

Freshness

New issues and status changes are easier to spot than stale background noise.

C

Action

Each item should point the operator back to the right place to fix it.

03 · Alert and learn

Be notified without living in the dashboard.

The dashboard gives you health, history, and context. Email is for the moments that deserve a human’s attention.

Health◔

Account health at a glance

Understand whether the account is quiet, changing, or asking for action.

History↺

Issues over time

See what was detected, when it appeared, and whether it is still active.

Alerts⌁

Useful email, quieter inbox

Get a concise summary when the monitored state changes in a meaningful way.

What MerchantAlert deliberately does not doIt is not a feed manager, catalog editor, bid tool, or replacement for Merchant Center. The promise is narrower: catch important issues early and help the right person act.
