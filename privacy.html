Last reviewed: 28 September 2026

What this site currently does

The current build is a static product website for MerchantAlert, a product in validation. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics, an embedded chat widget, a CRM, or a payment processor. The contact form is intentionally inert until an owner-controlled inbox or form endpoint is configured.

Data that may be processed after launch configuration

If the owner enables the contact flow, the site may receive the information a visitor chooses to submit, such as name, work email, store or agency name, and message content. The final notice must state why the data is collected, the lawful basis, who receives it, where it is processed, and how long it is kept.

Data minimisation

Do not ask for Merchant Center IDs, API keys, OAuth secrets, service-account files, payment details, identity documents, or customer data in a marketing contact form. Those items belong in a controlled onboarding process with its own security and contractual review.

Rights and contact

The final operator must publish a real privacy contact and a process for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection requests where applicable. No invented legal entity or email is included in this draft.

Why the draft is conservative

European Commission guidance describes transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, and integrity/confidentiality as core data-protection principles. MerchantAlert should complete the final notice only after the actual processing design, vendors, business identity, and jurisdictions are known.

Review the European Commission’s GDPR principles before finalising the public notice.
