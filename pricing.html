What you are buying

A monitoring layer, not a feed manager.

Does MerchantAlert edit products or feeds?

No. The focus is detecting and prioritizing Merchant Center issues. The source-of-truth changes remain in Shopify, Merchant Center, or the team’s existing workflow.

Can I use it for multiple client stores?

The agency tier is a conversation about that exact workflow. The final store limits and permissions should be agreed before a pilot starts.

When does billing start?

Not from this static site. Early access is manual until the owner has finalized identity, terms, privacy, payment processing, and support operations.

Why are the prices in euros?

They are a provisional reference point for validation. The owner should confirm the operating currency, tax treatment, and invoice requirements before launch.
