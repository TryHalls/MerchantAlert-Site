Product description

MerchantAlert is intended to monitor Google Merchant Center issues for Shopify stores. The service is not a feed manager, catalog editor, advertising platform, legal adviser, or guarantee that Google will approve products or preserve traffic.

Validation and early access

Early access conversations are for product discovery and pilot scoping. A request for access is not an acceptance of a paid order. Any paid service must be governed by a separate agreement or final terms published by the operator.

Availability and changes

The final terms should explain service availability, maintenance, reconciliation timing, alert delivery limits, supported integrations, suspension, termination, and how material changes are communicated.

Customer responsibilities

Customers must use accounts and permissions they control or are authorised to manage. They must not send secrets, identity documents, payment details, or personal data that is not necessary for the agreed workflow.

Commercial items still to decide

  • Legal contracting entity and jurisdiction.
  • Currency, taxes, invoicing, renewals, refunds, and cancellation.
  • Support response expectations and pilot success criteria.
  • Data-processing agreement, subprocessors, deletion, and security commitments.

These choices require the owner’s business and legal decisions. MerchantAlert should not present provisional copy as a binding contract.
